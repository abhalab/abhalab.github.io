Principal investigator
AM

Dr. Abhishek Kumar Mishra

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Jammu

Abhi works on privacy and security in networked systems and data releases, with a focus on mobility and wireless data. Before joining IIT Jammu he was a postdoctoral researcher at KIT (Germany) and Inria Lyon (France), and a visiting scientist at Northeastern University (USA). He received his PhD from Institut Polytechnique de Paris (École Polytechnique / Inria Saclay), his master's from KTH and Sorbonne, and his BTech in Electrical Engineering from IIT Mandi.

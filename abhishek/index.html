About

Hi, I’m Abhishek (Abhi) Mishra.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Jammu, where I lead ABHA Lab (Anonymity for Bharat).

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Chair of Privacy and Security, Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), Germany, working with Prof. Thorsten Strufe. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the PRIVATICS team at Inria Lyon, where I worked with Mathieu Cunche.

I work across disciplines and am always open to discussing new research ideas, collaborations, and opportunities. Students interested in working with me should read how to join ABHA Lab.

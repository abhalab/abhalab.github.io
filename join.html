Join us

We are building the group now, which means early members shape what it becomes.

Who we're looking for

Students who like taking systems apart. Useful backgrounds include networking, security, machine learning, statistics, signal processing and optimisation. You don't need prior privacy research; you do need to be comfortable writing code and reading papers.

PhD

Apply through IIT Jammu's PhD admission cycle in CSE and mention ABHA Lab in your statement of purpose. Email us before you apply so we can discuss a topic.

MTech

MTech students in CSE can do their thesis with the lab. Talk to us in your first semester so the project has time to become a paper.

BTech and interns

IIT Jammu BTech students can join for projects or summer work. A limited number of external interns may be considered; say which theme interests you and why.

How to apply

Email join@abha-lab.in with the subject line [ABHA] PhD, [ABHA] MTech or [ABHA] Intern. Attach your CV and transcripts, and write a few lines about one paper from our publications page: what it did, and what you would try next.

That last part matters most. Generic emails don't get replies.

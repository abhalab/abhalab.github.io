Fit

Who we're looking for

Students who like taking systems apart.

You don't need prior privacy research; you do need to be comfortable writing code and reading papers. Useful backgrounds include:

  • Networking
  • Security
  • Machine learning
  • Statistics
  • Signal processing
  • Optimisation

Process

How to apply

  1. Read one of our papers

    Pick one from our publications and write a few lines: what it did, and what you would try next.

  2. Email us

    Write to join@abha-lab.in with the subject line [ABHA] PhD, [ABHA] MTech or [ABHA] Intern.

  3. Attach your documents

    Include your CV and transcripts.

The note on a paper matters most. Generic emails don't get replies.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior research experience in privacy?

No. You do need to be comfortable writing code and reading papers. Backgrounds in networking, security, machine learning, statistics, signal processing or optimisation are all useful.

I want to do a PhD. When should I contact you?

Before you apply. Admission is through IIT Jammu's PhD admission cycle in CSE; email us first so we can discuss a topic, and mention ABHA Lab in your statement of purpose.

I'm an MTech student at IIT Jammu. When should I reach out?

In your first semester, so the project has time to become a paper.

I'm not at IIT Jammu. Can I intern with the lab?

A limited number of external interns may be considered. In your email, say which research theme interests you and why.

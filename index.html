ABHA Lab आभा

Anonymity for Bharat

We study how personal data leaks from the systems India runs on — transport and mobility records, phones and wireless devices, health data, payments — and we build protections that hold up when someone actually tries to break them.

A privacy and security research group in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Jammu.

What we work on

Mobility and location data

Re-identifying people in released trajectory and origin–destination data, and building anonymisation that scales to millions of users.

Wireless tracking

How Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and LoRaWAN devices give away identity and location, and how people can detect trackers aimed at them.

Anonymisation that holds up

Testing whether privacy claims survive real attacks, and turning the results into guidance for data publishers and regulators.

Network and IoT security

Measuring what devices and attackers actually do on the network, from DNS in smart homes to scanning on the open web.
